Glossary

Version types

The same movie can exist in several audio masters. This site tracks dialogue that changed between them — not every missing shot or trimmed fight.

TV

Broadcast / basic cable

Swears are muted, bleeped, or replaced with a new line recorded later (ADR). “Mr. Falcon” and “stranger in the Alps” live here.

Example: Die Hard 2

Physical Media

4K, Blu-Ray, DVD, or other

Studios or directors strip language, sex, and sometimes plane-crash talk. Dialogue is often redubbed or removed.

Example: Pleasantville on 4K has a line removed

Streaming

Platform master

A service ships a TV master by mistake, or quietly swaps a word in a documentary. These changes can appear and disappear without a note.

Example: Free Solo on Disney+

Ratings cut

Board / market cut

A line is changed to hit PG-13 instead of R, or to pass a national board. Sometimes only a few words move and the runtime barely changes.

Example: BBFC dialogue substitutions

4K UHD

HDR/Dolby Vision disc

often a new master, sometimes a different cut than the old Blu-ray.

Example: Die Hard 2

Blu-ray

HD disc

compare edition to edition, not “Blu-ray” as a single thing.

Example: Pleasantville on 4K has a line removed

DVD

SD disc

older transfers, TV masters, and alternate cuts show up here a lot.

Example: Free Solo on Disney+

VHS

Analog tape

pan-and-scan and TV audio are common.

Example: BBFC dialogue substitutions

Replaced

A new word or sentence is dubbed over the original.

Deleted

The line is cut. You hear a jump or see a short visual skip.

Muted

The mouth still moves; the audio is silenced or bleeped.
