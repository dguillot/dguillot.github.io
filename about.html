About

Who am I?

I’m Danny, the person behind Wrasslin War Room (WWRzone). You know me as the Frenchman who talks about horror movies, posts dated clips from Mid-Atlantic (NWA) through the Attitude Era (WWE), and is always on the hunt for wrestling cards. It Used To Say grew out of that naturally, after I heard a lot of my favourite creators talking about censorship in current 4K releases.

What this site is

It Used To Say tracks dialogue that changed: replaced words, muted swears, and deleted lines. Each entry should name the movie or video, the speaker, the original line, what replaced it (or that it was removed), the version type, and a source.

What I do not do

  • Host movie files or clips.
  • Claim a streaming copy is “wrong” as a legal matter — only that it differs.
